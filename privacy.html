Sans Comic

Privacy

The comic you write stays in this browser. This page says what that means.

16 November 2026

What stays on this device

  • Strips, the cast, uploaded backdrop photos, and the writing-desk size are saved in this browser's local storage.
  • They stay here until you delete them, clear the browser's site data, or save a backup or a project file.
  • A backup of My comics, a project file, PNG, GIF, PDF, or script is downloaded to your device. Sans Comic does not upload it.

What the server sees

  • The page, pictures, and fonts are files. Opening the site does not send your comic.
  • There is no account. A visit is counted by analytics we run ourselves at analytics.manaiakalani.info.
  • That count includes the page address, the site that linked you here, your browser, device, and screen size, plus a country and region estimated from the IP address. The address itself is not kept. There is no analytics cookie. A click that leaves the site, such as the GitHub link, is counted too.
  • The comic, its title, the line you are typing, and any backdrop photo stay in this browser. They are not part of the page address. The counter is not set to record typing, copied text, button labels, or a replay of the page.
  • The host that serves the page, and Cloudflare in front of comic.tinyinternet.company, can see ordinary connection details such as IP address, browser, and the time of the request. Those logs are not your strip.

Draw together

On this site the shared-room control is off. If a host turns it on, the lines you send in a room go to the other people in that room. The room does not keep its own copy. Each person still has the strip in their own browser.

Pictures you upload

A backdrop photo is cropped on this device and stored with the strip. It is not sent anywhere unless you export a project file and share that file yourself.
